About Us

Our Story, Our Purpose

Established in 2019, Bhavprita Seva Samiti started with a simple yet powerful vision — every community deserves access to education, every woman deserves the power to shape her destiny, and every child deserves to grow up surrounded by nature's bounty.

  • Growing from 1 village to 12+ rural and tribal communities
  • Holistic approach addressing environment, education & gender equality
  • Community-led programs, driven by local voices
  • Transparent operations — every rupee accountable

Our Mission

To empower rural communities through education, environmental stewardship, and inclusive development programs.

Our Vision

A self-reliant, educated, and environmentally conscious society where every individual thrives with dignity.

Founder's Message

"Every tree we plant, every student we encourage, every woman we empower — these aren't just programs. They are seeds of a revolution that will bloom for generations to come."

Mohini Kumari
Founder & President, Bhavprita Seva Samiti
Our Programs

What We Do

Five focused programs creating multi-dimensional impact across rural and tribal communities of India.

Environmental Protection & Awareness

Tree plantation drives, waste management workshops, and environmental awareness campaigns in schools and communities. 15,000+ trees planted, 5 community gardens created.

Adult Education · प्रौढ़ शिक्षा

Literacy classes for adults in rural communities — reading, writing, numeracy, and digital literacy. Over 800 adults have become literate through our centers.

Literacy Numeracy Digital Skills

Student Motivation Program

Career counseling sessions, mentorship, and inspirational talks at government schools encouraging students to pursue higher education and break cycles of poverty.

Matric Topper Awards

Honoring and rewarding top-performing students of government school matric examinations with cash prizes, medals, and certificates to motivate academic excellence.

Cash Prizes Medals Certificates

Tribal Women Empowerment

Skill development, self-help group (SHG) formation, vocational training, and leadership programs helping tribal women achieve financial independence and social dignity.
